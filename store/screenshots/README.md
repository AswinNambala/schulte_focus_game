# Google Play Store Screenshots Guide

Google Play requires at least 2 screenshots (up to 8 per device type). Recommended resolution for phones: **1080 x 2400** (or 1080 x 1920) in 24-bit PNG or JPEG.

### Recommended Screenshot Sequence:

1. **Screenshot 1 — Classic Schulte Table Mode**
   - **Page:** `GamePage` (Classic 5x5 Number Grid)
   - **Caption/Headline:** "TRAIN PERIPHERAL VISION — Master classic Schulte tables with dynamic cell reshuffling."
   - **Visual highlights:** Target indicator at top (Next: 7), large timer, vibrant cell colors.

2. **Screenshot 2 — 3D Concentric Ring Mode**
   - **Page:** `GamePage` (Circular 3D Mode)
   - **Caption/Headline:** "IMMERSIVE 3D RINGS — Track numbers in concentric orbits with touch rotation & depth scaling."
   - **Visual highlights:** 3D perspective tilted disc, cell pop animations, rotation indicator.

3. **Screenshot 3 — 20 Adaptive Challenge Levels**
   - **Page:** `LevelsPage`
   - **Caption/Headline:** "ADAPTIVE DIFFICULTY — 20 progressive levels scaling speed, grid sizes, and distractor angles."
   - **Visual highlights:** Star rating unlocks (1-3 stars), level grid, difficulty badges.

4. **Screenshot 4 — In-Depth Reaction Analytics**
   - **Page:** `ReportsPage`
   - **Caption/Headline:** "ACTIONABLE INSIGHTS — Track average reaction speed per tap and daily practice streaks."
   - **Visual highlights:** Daily play time bar chart (7D/30D), completion speed progress line chart, best records.

5. **Screenshot 5 — Personalized Training & Accessibility**
   - **Page:** `SettingsPage`
   - **Caption/Headline:** "TAILORED FOR YOU — Material 3 Dark/Light themes, colorblind-friendly palette & haptics."
   - **Visual highlights:** Theme mode segmented button, haptic feedback toggle, daily streak badge.
