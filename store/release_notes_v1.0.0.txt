Welcome to the initial production release of Schulte Focus (v1.0.0)!

What's New:
• Classic Schulte Grid Mode with customizable 3x3 to 7x7 layouts.
• Innovative 3D Concentric Ring Mode with touch-drag rotation and depth-based cell pop animations.
• Adaptive difficulty system across 20 progressive challenge levels.
• Rich performance analytics: progress line charts, daily play bar charts, and average reaction time per tap.
• Offline gameplay with automatic cloud synchronization.
• Material 3 UI with dark/light themes, colorblind-friendly palette, and tactile haptic feedback.
• Account management with Google Sign-In and full data deletion support.
