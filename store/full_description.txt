WHY TRAIN WITH SCHULTE FOCUS?
Originally invented by German psychiatrist Walter Schulte, Schulte tables are widely used by pilots, speed readers, competitive athletes, and memory champions to train peripheral perception, reduce eye saccades, and eliminate mental tunnel vision.

Whether preparing for exams, boosting reading comprehension speed, or maintaining razor-sharp daily focus, a 5-minute session per day delivers noticeable improvements in visual attention.
