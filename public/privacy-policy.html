Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Schulte Focus mobile application (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), published under package name com.aswinnambala.schulte_focus on Google Play. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparent handling of your data.

1. Information We Collect

To provide core focus training features, synchronization, and performance reports, we collect the following:

  • Account Information: When you create an account via Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we store your email address, display name, and unique user ID (UID). Passwords are encrypted and handled exclusively by Google Firebase Authentication; we never store plain-text passwords.
  • Gameplay & Focus Metrics: Game completion times, table configurations (grid size, ring count, table type), tap reaction times, error counts, level unlocks, and daily streaks are stored to calculate your progress charts.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Reports: Anonymous crash reports and performance data collected via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs and enhance stability.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Authenticating your account and synchronizing progress across your devices.
  • Calculating adaptive difficulty levels (stars, leveling up or down).
  • Rendering your visual reaction charts and daily streak analytics.
  • Detecting and resolving software crashes.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is processed solely through trusted Google Firebase infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: User identity management.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Encrypted cloud database for game progress.
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics: App diagnostic metrics.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL. Database access is strictly governed by Firebase Security Rules ensuring that users can only access their own records.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. You can do this directly inside the app under Profile > Delete Account, or online via our Account Deletion Page.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@aswinnambala.com.
