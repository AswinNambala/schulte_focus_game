Google Play Data Safety & Deletion Policy

User Account & Data Deletion Request

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy on Account Deletion, users of Schulte Focus (Package: com.aswinnambala.schulte_focus) have full control over their account and personal data. You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can instantly delete your account and all stored cloud records directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Schulte Focus application on your Android device.
  2. Sign in to your account if prompted.
  3. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Scroll down to the Account section.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm by entering your password (if applicable) and tapping Permanently Delete.

All data is wiped instantly from our Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore servers.

Method 2: Web-Based Data Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit a deletion request via email:

Submit Deletion Request by Email:
To: support@aswinnambala.com
Subject: [Account Deletion Request] Schulte Focus
Please include the email address registered with your Schulte Focus account.

What Data Is Deleted?

When an account deletion request is processed, the following data is permanently purged:

  • Authentication Profile: User UID, email address, display name, and authentication tokens in Firebase Authentication.
  • User Document: Profile records stored in Cloud Firestore under users/{uid}.
  • Game & Analytics History: All Schulte table sessions, completion times, reaction speed logs, errors, and daily streaks in users/{uid}/sessions/*.
  • Local Cache: Local cached session logs and level unlock history stored on the device.
Permanent Action: Once deleted, your account and training statistics cannot be recovered.

Data Retention Period

In-app requests take effect immediately. Web or email requests are verified and processed within 48 hours of verification. No user records or analytics logs are retained after deletion.
